RFC-0038 step 1: `Site.collections.<key>.permalink` in the contracts (a template, `/blog/{slug}`; v1 is fixed segments plus `{slug}`), and `entryPath(site, entry)` / `entryAt(site, path)` in `@cloudhal/core-ts` (also `@cloudhal/core-ts/permalinks`). Every entry URL in the renderer, starter, API, MCP server, CLI and Studio now comes from them; with no `collections` set, every URL is the same as before. The renderer's one-argument `entryPath(entry)` is replaced by core's `entryPath(site, entry)` (re-exported from `@cloudhal/astro-renderer/routing`); themes pass the site, or `MediaContext.permalinks`.
