Studio links to the live site: a "View site" button in the top bar (also in the site switcher and the ⌘K palette), "Live at" and "Last published" on the overview, "View live" and "Copy link" on published pages and articles, and the full address under the slug and path fields ("Live at" when the live snapshot serves it, "Will be at" until then). After a site publish the toast offers "View site" and "Copy link", as do rollback and approval toasts. Marking a page or article published now says it goes live with the next site publish, with a "Publish site" action, instead of "Page published".
