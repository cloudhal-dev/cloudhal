Collections stage 3 in the renderer (RFC-0042 step 3, ADR-0044). A collection's `expiry` gives every entry a derived gate (`min(availability.until, field date + grace)`; a date ends at the next day's start in the site's zone), applied by both published sources at render time: `unlist` keeps the entry's page but leaves `entries()`, `allEntries()`, term filters and archives, related posts, feeds, the sitemap, `llms.txt` and search (search rows' `gateUntil`); `hide` answers 404 like an expired `availability`. The gate index adds every derived instant and every value instant of date/datetime list fields, so the edge cache expires there without a purge; previews ignore derived gates and say "Ended on …". New theme APIs: `GraphSource.queryEntries` and `queryEntries(source, q)` (`./queries`) list a collection by its list fields (`where`, `orderBy`, `sort`, `filter`, `limit`, `offset`, `includeExpired`) with one evaluator on live and snapshot sources; `contentListEntries`; `GraphSource.routableEntries` (entries with a public URL, unlisted included); `withStructuredData` and `entryStructuredData` (`./seo`) replace the generic `BlogPosting` with the collection's JSON-LD preset when the entry has every required value. `ContentList.astro` honours `where`/`orderBy`, also for theme copies that pass a source's list. `entryHead` is unchanged, so old themes keep today's output; the blog starter calls `withStructuredData` and `contentListEntries`. The site Worker's `GET /__cloudhal/features` answers `{ "gates": 2 }`, and writers (store, API, MCP, bundle import) refuse a new or changed `expiry` with `409 gates_unsupported` until it does (`GateSupport.enforced(siteId, version)`, `EXPIRY_GATES_VERSION`). A live entry whose published change adds a derived gate or moves it earlier is never low risk in `auto` mode (`expiringEntries`, auto-check code `expires`, for publishes and releases). Sites without the new settings render as before.
