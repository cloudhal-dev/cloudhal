Icons in surfaces (RFC-0026 26b).
- **Catalog.** An `Icon` component; `icon` on Button (with `iconPosition`), Badge and Stat; any icon as a List marker.
- **Formats.** `format: "icon"` and `format: "marker"` in the props schema subset.
- **Repairs and A2UI.** Model spellings are repaired to the set's names. A2UI `Icon` imports and exports.
