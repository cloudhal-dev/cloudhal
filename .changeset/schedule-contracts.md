RFC-0008 draft contracts for schedules, all additive (`schemaVersion` stays 1). `Site.timeZone` (an IANA name; absent means UTC). `availability` on `ContentEntry` and `Page`: time gates `from` (embargo) and `until` (expiry) that the published renderer checks at request time. Three new roots: `Release` (`release.schema.json`), a scheduled change set of node versions pinned by rev and hash with their live base, a state (`draft`, `awaiting_approval`, `scheduled`, `running`, `published`, `conflict`, `missed`, `expired`, `failed`, `canceled`), an `rl1:` approval binding, a person's or `auto`-mode approval and an outcome; `Automation` (`automation.schema.json`), a built-in deterministic job or a scheduled agent task with a recurrence in Cloudhal's RRULE subset, a mode cap that is never `bypass`, a run window and budgets; `ScheduleRun` (`schedule-run.schema.json`), one occurrence of either, with its trigger, lateness, budget usage, taint and outcome. Shared `$defs` `TimeZone`, `LocalDateTime` and `Availability` join `common.schema.json`. `assertValid("release" | "automation" | "schedule-run", …)` and `ROOT_SCHEMAS` include them.
