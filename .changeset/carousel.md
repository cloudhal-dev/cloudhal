Carousel (RFC-0027 27a): a `Carousel` catalog component (hero and rail) that works without JavaScript. It is a scroll-snap track with named slides and link markers. The hero's first slide loads first. `render_page` adds the `carousel-label` and `carousel-lcp` checks.
